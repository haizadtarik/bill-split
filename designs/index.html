How to use these

  • All four use the same sample bill (Taverna 14, 4 diners) so you're comparing look & feel, not content.
  • These are static visual mockups — no real OCR or interactivity yet. They're for picking a direction.
  • My read: Modern Fintech is the safest base; Playful Social best fits the "casual friends" audience; Receipt Native is the most distinctive wedge. We can also mix — e.g. Fintech structure with Receipt-Native flourishes on the results screen.
  • Next step once you pick: lock the data model + tech stack and scaffold the MVP in the chosen style.
